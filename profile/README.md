#### LLM usage policy

Tomori Lab follows the principles of [Responsible Use of Generative AI](https://www.debian.org/vote/2026/vote_002), adopted by Debian in its 2026 General Resolution. All contributions must meet the same standards of quality, correctness, maintainability, and legal compliance, regardless of whether generative AI tools, including LLMs, were used.

- **Human review and accountability**: Contributors must understand and review AI-assisted output, perform the necessary tests, and make any required changes. Contributors remain responsible for the quality and suitability of everything they submit.
- **Copyright and licensing**: Contributors must check the provenance and licensing of their output and exclude material whose legal status they cannot reasonably justify. Existing copyright and licensing requirements apply regardless of the tools used.
- **Disclosure**: Contributors are encouraged to disclose AI assistance. This policy does not require disclosure.
- **Confidentiality and privacy**: Non-public code, private communications, undisclosed security vulnerabilities, cryptographic keys, credentials, and other sensitive material must not be shared with third-party AI services without explicit authorization and compliance with the project's security and privacy requirements.
- **Large-scale automation**: Bulk issue or patch submissions and extensive automated changes require prior discussion and agreement through the appropriate project channels. A human must oversee and remain accountable for the process and its output.
